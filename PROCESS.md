# Process

## Starting idea

I wanted to make a small, silly personality quiz that gives someone a quick break and a funny potato character as a result.

## Design changes

I used AI to help write the first version of the Python and Streamlit code. One person tried the early version and said it felt too much like a questionnaire and that the wording was too serious. In response, I changed the experience to show one question at a time and move on automatically after an answer, and rewrote the copy in a more absurd, playful tone. The later version added a falling-answer animation, optional collectible potato stamps, and illustrated results.

## AI use

I used ChatGPT/Codex to help draft and revise the code, interface copy, animation, and documentation. I kept the idea of using a falling-answer animation and optional stamps to make choices more playful. I changed the earlier questionnaire-like flow and serious copy in response to the tester's feedback.

## User trial

One person tried the early version. Their feedback was that the interface felt like a questionnaire and the copy was too serious. I changed the interaction to one question at a time with automatic progression, and made the wording more absurd and playful. The tester is not identified here.

## Credits

The quiz copy, code, and five potato result illustrations were made for this project with AI assistance. The app uses Python and Streamlit; dependencies are listed in `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock`.
